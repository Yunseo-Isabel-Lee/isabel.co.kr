About

SincereLee is a name that echoes “Sincerely,” the way a letter signs off.

sincērusSincerely comes from the Latin sincerus—“pure, without falsehood”—and since the early 1700s it has closed letters with the quiet promise, “this is heartfelt.”

Why “sincere,” of all words?

  1. 允윤Because the “yun” in my name, Yunseo, is 允—to be true.
  2. S서And among the many words for truth, I chose this one because its first letter, S, could hold “Seo” of my name.
  3. Lee이Joined with my family name, Lee, it reads aloud as sincerely—from the heart.

And so all three syllables of my name came to rest within a single word.
